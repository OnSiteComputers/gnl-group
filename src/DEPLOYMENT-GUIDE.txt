GNL DIGITAL GROUP - GO LIVE CHECKLIST
================================================================

STEP 1: PUBLISH YOUR APP
----------------------------------------------------------------
1. In Base44 editor, click "Publish" in the top right corner
2. Click "Publish App" to push your changes live
3. Your site is now live at your base44.app URL

================================================================
STEP 2: CONNECT YOUR GODADDY DOMAIN
================================================================

PART A: In Base44 Dashboard
----------------------------------------------------------------
1. Go to Dashboard → Domains
2. Click "Connect existing domain"
3. Enter: gnldigitalgroup.com
4. Keep this window open - you'll need to verify after DNS setup

PART B: In GoDaddy DNS Settings
----------------------------------------------------------------
1. Log into GoDaddy → My Products
2. Find your domain → Click "DNS" or "Manage DNS"

3. ADD these two DNS records:

   Type        | Host | Value                    | TTL
   ------------|------|--------------------------|------
   A           | @    | 216.24.57.1              | 600
   CNAME       | www  | base44.onrender.com      | 600

4. DELETE any existing:
   - A records pointing to other IP addresses
   - AAAA (IPv6) records
   - Conflicting CNAME records (like parking pages)

PART C: Back in Base44
----------------------------------------------------------------
1. Click "Verify" next to your domain name
2. Wait for DNS propagation:
   - Usually completes in 1-2 hours
   - Can take up to 48 hours maximum
3. SSL certificate will be issued automatically

================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================
If domain doesn't verify after 2 hours:
- Double-check DNS records in GoDaddy for typos
- Ensure no old A/AAAA records are still active
- Try clearing your browser cache
- Wait up to 48 hours for full DNS propagation

If you need help:
- Base44 Support: Check docs.base44.com
- GoDaddy Support: Available 24/7 via chat or phone
