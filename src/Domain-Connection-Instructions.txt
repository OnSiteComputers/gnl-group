==========================================================
HOW TO CONNECT YOUR GODADDY DOMAIN TO BASE44
==========================================================

STEP 1: IN BASE44 DASHBOARD
----------------------------------------------------------
1. Log into your Base44 app editor
2. Click "Dashboard" in the top menu
3. Click "Domains" in the left sidebar
4. Click "Connect existing domain"
5. Enter your domain name (example: gnldigitalgroup.com)
6. Click "Connect" or "Add Domain"


STEP 2: IN GODADDY
----------------------------------------------------------
1. Log into your GoDaddy account at godaddy.com
2. Go to "My Products"
3. Find your domain in the list
4. Click "DNS" or "Manage DNS" next to your domain
5. You'll see the DNS Management page


ADD THESE TWO DNS RECORDS:
----------------------------------------------------------

RECORD 1 - ROOT DOMAIN:
-----------------------
Type:     A
Host:     @
Value:    216.24.57.1
TTL:      Default (or 1 hour)

How to add in GoDaddy:
- Click "Add Record" or the "+" button
- Select "A" from the Type dropdown
- Enter "@" in the Host field
- Enter "216.24.57.1" in the Value field
- Leave TTL as default
- Click "Save" or "Add Record"


RECORD 2 - WWW SUBDOMAIN:
--------------------------
Type:     CNAME
Host:     www
Value:    base44.onrender.com
TTL:      Default (or 1 hour)

How to add in GoDaddy:
- Click "Add Record" or the "+" button
- Select "CNAME" from the Type dropdown
- Enter "www" in the Host field
- Enter "base44.onrender.com" in the Value field
- Leave TTL as default
- Click "Save" or "Add Record"


IMPORTANT - REMOVE CONFLICTING RECORDS:
----------------------------------------------------------
Before or after adding the new records, check for:

- Any existing A records pointing to other IP addresses
- Any AAAA records (IPv6 records) - these should be REMOVED
- Any CNAME records for "@" or "www" pointing elsewhere

Delete or update any conflicting records to avoid problems.


STEP 3: BACK IN BASE44
----------------------------------------------------------
1. Return to Base44 Dashboard → Domains
2. Find your domain in the list
3. Click "Verify" next to your domain
4. Wait for verification to complete


WHAT TO EXPECT:
----------------------------------------------------------
- DNS propagation can take 48-72 hours (usually faster)
- SSL certificate will be issued automatically once verified
- Your site will be live at your custom domain once complete
- You may see "Pending" or "Connecting" status initially


TROUBLESHOOTING:
----------------------------------------------------------
If your domain is stuck in "Pending" or "Connecting":
- Double-check that DNS records are entered correctly
- Make sure you removed any AAAA (IPv6) records
- Wait a bit longer for DNS propagation
- Try clearing your browser cache
- Contact Base44 support if issues persist
